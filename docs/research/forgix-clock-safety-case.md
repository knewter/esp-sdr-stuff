The first field deliberately reviews **assumptions** for a bounded diagnostic;
requiring a prior measured clock would create a bootstrap cycle. It does not
permit guessing pin ownership or voltage. Part/oscillator uncertainty may be
declared only with an independent safety rationale and a conservative supported
envelope. Unsupported electrical/reset assumptions stay unresolved. A subsequent
physical result must retain failures and cannot retroactively justify access.

An observer capture can stop the one-shot1024-period source after16 samples;
it is not natural-source completion or continuous timing. PIO checks nominal
`clk_sys=150MHz`, divider1; neither that check nor the FPGA32MHz compile model
calibrates actual frequency. Digital `[2n−16,2n+16]` bounds exclude unverified
variable pad/synchronizer delay and aliasing. Interpret no ratio as physical
frequency until the independent envelope is supported.
